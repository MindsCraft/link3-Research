# Link3 UX & Product Research Repository

Welcome to the Link3 UX Research workspace. This directory serves as the single source of truth for competitive benchmarking, usability audits, customer journeys, and interaction design principles.

---

## 📁 Research Workflow & Directory Structure

Follow this sequence to maintain consistent research direction from discovery to execution:

```text
research/
├── 01-benchmarks/   # Phase 1: Market discovery & competitor teardowns (50 Competitors + Strategic Blueprint v1.1)
├── 02-personas/     # Phase 2: User archetypes, pain points, and customer journey maps (5 Archetypes)
├── 03-audits/       # Phase 3: Heuristic evaluations, accessibility, and current-state friction audits (10 NN/g Principles)
├── 04-insights/     # Phase 4: Synthesized UX decisions, component specs, and 6-sprint engineering backlog
└── 05-wireframes/   # Phase 5: Structural layout blueprints, responsive desktop/mobile wireframes & interaction specs
```

---

## 🎯 Primary Research Objectives

1. **Information Architecture (IA)**:
   - Clarify the separation between Home, Enterprise, and Wholesale segments.
   - Streamline access to high-frequency actions (Recharge / Bill Pay, Coverage Check, Self-Care).

2. **Conversion & Onboarding**:
   - Simplify broadband plan selection (speed, OTT bundles, router tiers, pricing transparency).
   - Frictionless coverage verification and lead capture.

3. **Interaction & Motion Quality**:
   - Benchmark against modern interaction standards (Originkit, Linear, Apple, modern SaaS).
   - Move from generic/clunky transitions to physics-based, purposeful micro-animations.

4. **Trust & Reliability Signals**:
   - Highlight network superiority (100% full-fibre, BDIX peering, low gaming ping, 24/7 dedicated support).
