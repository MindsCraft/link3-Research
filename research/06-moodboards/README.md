# Phase 6: Visual Mood Boards & Creative Direction

This directory contains the visual design system mood boards, brand aesthetic specifications, competitor-derived design directions, and interactive visual prototypes for the Link3 digital web experience platform.

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Competitor Lineage

Building upon the **34-Competitor Benchmark Research** (`01-benchmarks`), Customer Personas (`02-personas`), Heuristic Audits (`03-audits`), and Structural Wireframes (`05-wireframes`), Phase 6 establishes the high-fidelity visual identity for the Link3 web experience.

The visual direction directly synthesizes best practices from the world's most innovative broadband and digital telco providers across **17 High-Density Emerging Market Leaders** (India, Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand) and **17 World-Class Global Altnet Disruptors** (USA, UK, France, Malaysia, Singapore, Europe):

* **TIME dotCom (Malaysia):** High-voltage typography, playful micro-interactions, 100% pure fiber branding.
* **Google Fiber (USA):** Radical simplicity, zero cognitive friction, instant 1-click address coverage verification in the hero.
* **Free (France - Freebox Ultra):** Sculpted hardware as luxury design furniture (Wi-Fi 7, NVMe SSD slot), all-inclusive streaming ecosystem bundles.
* **Airtel Black & JioFiber (India):** Dynamic "Build Your Own Bundle" configurator, 14+ OTT aggregator platforms, live pincode coverage check.
* **Converge ICT (Philippines):** Breakthrough "Time of Day" dynamic speed allocation (Day vs Night bandwidth boost).
* **Community Fibre London (UK):** London's fastest full-fiber, 4.9 Trustpilot trust badges, transparent pricing with no mid-contract inflation hikes.
* **MyRepublic & EE (UK/Singapore):** Esports gamer telemetry hubs, real-time BDIX/Singapore ping monitors, and smart home Wi-Fi modes.
