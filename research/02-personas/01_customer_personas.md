> *"When my ping spikes from 28ms to 120ms during a clutch round in Valorant, my rank is gone. If customer care tells me to restart my router one more time, I will lose my mind."*

#### A. Digital Behavior & Technical Fluency:
- Knows what BDIX peering is, monitors ping, jitter, and packet loss using NetLimiter and in-game overlays.
- Downloads 60GB–120GB game updates (Call of Duty, GTA) and gets furious when throttle limits or CDN routing bottlenecks choke download speeds to 2 MB/s.
- Active on Discord gaming servers; is the **de facto tech advisor** for his entire extended family and apartment building.

#### B. Core Pain Points & Friction:
1. **Unpredictable Peering & Route Jitter:** The ISP routes traffic through bad transit pipes during international peak hours, causing ping spikes to Singapore and Middle East game clusters.
2. **Insultingly Incompetent Tier-1 Support:** When he calls support to report packet loss on a specific IP hop, the call center agent asks if the green light is blinking on the router and says "Restart koren sir".
3. **CGNAT & Port Forwarding Blockers:** Stuck behind a carrier-grade NAT that causes Strict NAT type on consoles and prevents hosting game lobbies without paying expensive hidden charges for a Real Public IP.

#### C. Link3 Ecosystem Fit & Revenue Potential:
- **Primary Package:** **Link3 Ultra (500 Mbps Gamer Edition)**
- **Value-Added Add-on 1:** **Gaming Booster QoS Pack (৳99/mo):** Dynamic BGP route optimization prioritizing Valve, Riot, AWS Singapore, and Garena nodes, **including 1 free Public Static IPv4 address** for unlocked Open NAT and game server hosting (no hidden charges or nickel-and-diming).
- **Value-Added Add-on 2:** **Hardware Financing (Link3 EasyPay):** ASUS ROG Wi-Fi 6 Gaming Router or PlayStation 5 Slim on 12-to-24-month zero-interest installments (৳450–৳2,850/mo).
- **Trust Hook:** **Live Homepage Game Ping Matrix:** Real-time public ping ticker directly to Singapore AWS (ap-southeast-1), Valorant SG, and Steam BDIX.
- **Advocacy Value:** Converting Shakib turns him into an evangelist who influences 10–20 families in his building and gaming circle to switch to Link3.

2. **Micro-Drops During Client Calls:** 15-second fiber drops that disconnect Zoom calls. Even if it reconnects quickly, it destroys professional credibility with international clients.
3. **Unannounced Maintenance:** ISPs performing line maintenance at 11 PM without advance warning, precisely when US/European business days are in full swing.

- **Value-Added Add-on 2:** **Link3 CloudVault Storage (500GB/1TB):** Locally hosted BDIX high-speed cloud backup for creative assets (instant upload speeds compared to overseas cloud).
- **Value-Added Add-on 3:** **Pro SLA Notification System:** Real-time SMS/WhatsApp proactive alerts before scheduled maintenance, with compensation credits for unexpected downtime.
- **Willingness to Pay:** High. Nusrat will happily pay ৳2,500–৳3,500/month if reliability is rock-solid because her livelihood depends on it.
