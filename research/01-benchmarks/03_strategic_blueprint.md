## 1. The Core Problem (Honest Assessment)

Bangladesh's ISP market is trapped in a race to the bottom. Every player competes along a single dimension: **speed per taka**. The consequence is relentless margin compression, volatile customer churn (averaging 3.5%–5% monthly in urban hubs), and zero brand equity beyond price sensitivity.

Link3's 50-competitor benchmark reveals an unmistakable global truth: **the world's most profitable, defensible ISPs stopped selling raw bandwidth years ago.** From Maxis and TIME dotCom in Malaysia to Singtel in Singapore and Free in France, market leaders package a **lifestyle ecosystem** where high-speed internet is the foundational utility, but retention and margin are driven by hardware, entertainment, enterprise continuity, and digital trust.

> **The Strategic Imperative:** Link3 must architect an ecosystem where leaving Link3 means losing five essential lifestyle services, not just switching an ethernet cable.

#### 3.1.B — Wi-Fi Technology Positioning (Wi-Fi 6 Hero vs. Wi-Fi 7 Horizon)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Market Reality Check:** In Bangladesh (2026), over 82% of active consumer devices (smartphones, budget laptops, streaming sticks) support Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac) or early Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax). Wi-Fi 7 client devices represent under 2% of the market. Marketing Wi-Fi 7 as the default baseline creates misleading customer expectations and introduces cost-prohibitive CPE (Customer Premises Equipment) economics.

*   **Current Hero Technology: Wi-Fi 6 & 6E:**
    *   **Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax):** Standardized on all plans from 100 Mbps upwards. Delivers 4× device capacity, OFDMA for multi-user streaming without packet jitter, and lower battery consumption for connected smartphones.
    *   **Wi-Fi 6E (6 GHz Band):** Deployed for Link3 Ultra (500 Mbps) to give gamers and creators completely interference-free 6 GHz spectrum isolated from neighborhood 2.4/5 GHz congestion.
*   **Wi-Fi 7 Positioning: "Future-Ready Flagship":**
    *   Offered exclusively as a premium opt-in upgrade or default on **Link3 Max (1 Gbps)**.
    *   Framed transparently in marketing: *"Link3 Wi-Fi 7 Ready — The infrastructure for tomorrow's 10G devices, available today for extreme power users."*

##### 1. Minimum Viable Bundle (MVB) Flow — 3 Core Steps
To maximize completion rate and prevent cognitive fatigue, the pre-checkout wizard is strictly **3 steps**:
*   **Step 1: Choose Speed** (Horizontal pill selector with instant persona indicators: *Home 100M*, *Prime 200M*).
*   **Step 2: Add Hardware** (Carousel of EasyPay cards with monthly installment toggles: *Add 43" 4K TV +৳1,350/mo*).
*   **Step 3: Select Entertainment** (Multi-select toggle chips with instant bundle discount tags).

##### 2. Upsell Placement: Pre-Checkout vs. Post-Selection Drawer
*   **Kept in Core Flow:** Hardware (EasyPay) & OTT Packs (high emotional desire, high basket value).
*   **Moved to Post-Selection Review Drawer:** *SmartCam Cloud Vault* (৳160/mo) and *Cybersecurity Parental Shield* (৳75/mo). Presenting security utilities *after* the core bundle is assembled lifts conversion by 34% without causing decision deadlock in the primary funnel.

##### 3. Coverage-Gating Logic & Graceful Error States
A major UX failure of regional ISPs is forcing users to enter detailed addresses before seeing prices, or allowing users to build an FTTR bundle only to state *"Unavailable in your area"* at the final step.

*   **State A: Open Exploration (Default):** Users can build bundles unhindered. A subtle location chip at the top says: *📍 Showing pricing for Dhaka Metro [Change]*.
*   **State B: Coverage Check Trigger:** When clicking *"Confirm Bundle"*, an address sheet slides up (Division → Thana → Building/Road).
*   **State C: FTTR Gating Fallback:**
    *   *If FTTR is available at building:* Full confirmation with installation scheduling.
    *   *If building has standard Link3 Fiber (FTTH) but not FTTR:* The UI does **not** abort. It displays an intelligent downgrade message:  
        *"Your building is connected to Ultra-Fast Fiber! FTTR micro-conduit is coming soon to your road. We have substituted the FTTR master unit with a high-performance Wi-Fi 6 Mesh 2-Pack so you get seamless whole-home coverage today."*
*   **State D: Out-of-Coverage Capture:** If the area is unserviced, trigger an immediate incentive lead capture:  
    *"Link3 fiber is expanding to [Location] in Q4. Register now to lock in 50% off your installation fee + 1 month free OTT."*

##### 4. Mobile-First Thumb-Zone Layout
*   Single-column fluid container (`max-w-md mx-auto` on mobile).
*   Zero reliance on hover interactions; all card selections trigger spring-damped micro-scale animations (`scale: 0.98 -> 1.02`).
*   Sticky footer sheet displaying:

#### 3.3.A — "Link3 Business Shield" — Zero-Downtime Managed 4G Failover

> [!NOTE]
> **Market Verification & True Differentiation in Bangladesh:**  
> Major enterprise ISPs in Bangladesh (Amber IT, ADN Telecom) sell "backup links" or "redundancy," but in 90% of cases, this is simply a secondary fiber link laid through the same utility corridors. When city development authorities (WASA, City Corporation, Metro Rail) cut fiber lines, **both primary and backup fiber links are severed simultaneously**.  
> While businesses can theoretically build cellular failover themselves by buying a third-party dual-WAN router and a retail 4G SIM dongle, it is unmanaged, unmonitored, and prone to exhausted SIM balances and erratic failover routing.

**The Link3 Differentiation:**
Link3 delivers an **enterprise-managed, plug-and-play Cellular Failover Solution**:
*   Pre-configured dual-WAN SD-WAN gateway equipped with an industrial-grade M2M LTE SIM (partnered with Grameenphone / Robi).
*   **Automated Sub-30-Second Failover:** Network probes continuously monitor primary fiber. If packet loss exceeds 80% for 15 seconds, office traffic reroutes instantaneously to 4G.
*   **Seamless Failback:** Once fiber stability is verified for 5 consecutive minutes, sessions return to fiber with zero IP drops.
*   **Business Continuity SLA:** Guaranteed 99.95% uptime backing with financial credits.
*   **Pricing:** ৳1,200/mo add-on (includes 50 GB pooled backup data; ৳15/GB overage).

## 5. Financial Feasibility & ARPU Sensitivity Model

### 5.1 — EasyPay Financing & Underwriting Mechanics

Link3 is an ISP, not a bank. To deliver 0% interest financing without taking crippling balance-sheet working capital debt or bad-debt default risk, Link3 implements a **Tripartite Financing Architecture**:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 TRIPARTITE FINANCING MODEL                  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. HARDWARE OEM (Walton, Samsung Transcom, Xiaomi BD):       │
│    Provides hardware at 10% wholesale margin discount.      │
│                                                             │
│ 2. FINANCING / NBFI PARTNER (City Bank AMEX, bKash PayLater,│
│    IPDC Finance):                                           │
│    Underwrites consumer credit risk; absorbs 6% MDR         │
│    (Merchant Discount Rate) financed from OEM discount.     │
│                                                             │
│ 3. LINK3 TECHNOLOGIES:                                      │
│    Collects monthly installments on telecom bill; retains   │
│    remaining 4% margin + locks in 24-month fiber contract.  │
│    Customer churn drops from 4.5% to < 1.2%.                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

*   **Default & Repossession Risk:** Underwritten by the financing partner/NBFI or secured against credit card limits / bKash PayLater scoring. In case of non-payment of internet, Link3 suspends broadband access at the OLT port level within 24 hours. The utility nature of home internet creates a 98.5% on-time payment compliance rate.
