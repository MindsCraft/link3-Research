## 1. Executive Summary & Why These Markets Mirror Bangladesh

Broadband operators in South Asia and Southeast Asia operate in environments fundamentally identical to Bangladesh:
- **Massive Population Density:** Mega-cities (Mumbai, Jakarta, Manila, Hanoi, Bangkok) characterized by high-rise apartment clusters, narrow multi-story concrete townhouses, and dense commercial districts.
- **Prepaid Purchasing Psychology:** A consumer base accustomed to sachet data, mobile wallets (bKash/Nagad analogues: UPI, GoPay, GCash, MoMo), and resistance to rigid 24-month lock-in contracts.
- **Entertainment as the Anchor Tenant:** Raw bandwidth is a race-to-the-bottom commodity. Winning ISPs package unmetered broadband with **14+ OTT platform aggregators**, 4K Android TV streaming hubs, and exclusive live sports.
- **Physical Infrastructure Friction:** Power fluctuations, frequent optical fiber cuts due to urban road works, concrete wall Wi-Fi dead-zones, and reliance on decentralized Local Cable Operator (LCO) franchise networks.

The 17 benchmarked operators in this report demonstrate how market leaders have transformed from raw bit-pipes into high-ARPU **Digital Experience Platforms**.

## 2. Core Architectural Patterns in Emerging Market ISPs

### Pattern 1: Micro-Prepaid & Sachet Fiber Reloads (Globe At Home GFiber, Reliance Jio)
In markets with large freelance, gig-worker, student, and tenant populations, credit checks and annual commitments kill conversion. **Globe Philippines' GFiber Prepaid** allows users to buy full optical fiber access for 7 days or 15 days via mobile app with zero lock-in, driving explosive customer adoption without bad debt risk.

### Pattern 2: Multi-Story Concrete Townhouse Wi-Fi Solvers (Viettel, FPT, AIS Fibre3)
In Hanoi, Bangkok, and Dhaka, reinforced concrete construction destroys standard router signals between floors. **Viettel and AIS** bundle 2 to 3 pre-paired **Wi-Fi 6 mesh pods** directly into standard entry-level tiers (HOMET and FTTR glass packs) rather than treating them as expensive optional upsells.

### Pattern 3: OTT Super-App Aggregation (JioFiber, Airtel Xstream, Converge)
Instead of forcing consumers to manage individual subscriptions to Netflix, Disney+, and local regional platforms, **Airtel and Jio** integrate 15+ OTT services into a single unified bill with single sign-on (SSO) on a 4K set-top box.

### Pattern 4: Hybrid Franchise & LCO Decentralization (Alliance Broadband)
In regional secondary cities and mohallas, **Alliance Broadband (India)** scales by partnering with thousands of Local Cable Operators (LCOs) for last-mile drop fiber maintenance, while Alliance maintains central brand, core Tier-1 IP transit, and digital billing.

### Pattern 5: B2B Guaranteed CIR & Cloud Interconnect (RISE Fiber)
Corporate customers in developing economies fear fiber line cuts and oversold bandwidth. **RISE Fiber** guarantees 100% committed information rate (CIR) with direct cloud connections to AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure.

## 4. Key Recommendations for Link3's Web Experience Overhaul

1. **Adopt Converge's "Time-of-Day" or Dynamic Speed Allocator:** Allow users to prioritize daytime work bandwidth or nighttime streaming/gaming bandwidth.
2. **Launch "Sachet" Prepaid Fiber Trial Tiers:** Follow Globe Philippines' lead to capture the student and bachelor rental market without long-term contract lock-ins.
3. **Elevate Local OTT Bundling Front-and-Center:** Replicate Jio and FPT by prominently displaying Chorki, Hoichoi, Toffee, and SonyLIV logos directly on plan cards.
4. **Standardize on Bundled Wi-Fi 6 Mesh for Multi-Story Homes:** Directly solve Dhaka's concrete dead-zone pain point with clear multi-room coverage guarantees.
